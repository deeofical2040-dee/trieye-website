PROFESSIONAL CAR WASH

MORE THAN JUST WATER & SOAP

A proper car wash is about removing dirt safely while taking care of the vehicle's exterior surfaces. At TRIEYE, we use a structured washing process to clean common road dust, grime and contamination from your vehicle.

At our Villivakkam detailing studio, each wash is carried out with attention to body panels, wheels, glass and exterior finishing so your car leaves looking clean and refreshed.

OUR WASH PROCESS

A CAREFUL STEP-BY-STEP WASH

STEP 01

VEHICLE INSPECTION

Quick visual inspection of the exterior before washing.

STEP 02

PRE-RINSE & FOAM

Loose dirt is rinsed before applying foam across the exterior.

STEP 03

HAND WASH

STEP 04

WHEELS & DETAILS

STEP 05

RINSE, DRY & FINAL CHECK

Vehicle is rinsed, dried and visually checked before completion.

WHY FOAM WASH

LOOSEN DIRT BEFORE CONTACT

Foam helps cover the exterior surface before the main contact-wash stage. Combined with a careful wash process, it helps lift and loosen everyday surface dirt while preparing the vehicle for hand cleaning.
