INTERIOR CLEANING PROCESS

STAGE 02

VACUUM & DUST REMOVAL

Thorough vacuuming of carpets, seats, trunk, and crevices.

STAGE 03

STEAM & FOAM CLEANING

High-temperature steam and foaming agents to sanitize and loosen grime.

STAGE 04

LEATHER / FABRIC TREATMENT

Conditioning leather surfaces and deep extraction for fabric upholstery.

ODOR ELIMINATION & FINAL TOUCH

Air sanitization and final inspection of all interior glass and trim.
