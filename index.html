Frequently Asked Questions

For regular daily-use vehicles, professional detailing every 3–6 months helps maintain the exterior finish and interior condition. The ideal interval depends on usage, parking and driving conditions.

The durability depends on the coating package, vehicle usage and maintenance. Contact Trieye Studio for the recommended protection package for your vehicle.

PPF helps protect painted surfaces from minor scratches, stone chips, road debris and everyday wear while preserving the vehicle’s original finish.

Service time depends on the selected package and vehicle condition. Basic services may take a few hours, while intensive detailing and protection treatments can require more time.
