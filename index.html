Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers. A better detailing experience.

For regular daily-use vehicles, professional detailing every 3–6 months helps maintain the exterior finish and interior condition.

Ceramic coating durability depends on the coating package, vehicle usage and maintenance. Contact Trieye for the recommended protection option for your vehicle.

PPF helps protect painted surfaces from minor scratches, stone chips, road debris and everyday wear while preserving the vehicle's finish.

Service time depends on the selected package and vehicle condition. Basic services may take a few hours, while advanced detailing and protection services can take longer.

Booking in advance is recommended so we can prepare for your vehicle and selected service. Customers can book directly through the website.
