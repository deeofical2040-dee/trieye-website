GRAPHENE PROTECTION

ADVANCED PROTECTION FOR YOUR PAINT

Graphene coating is a liquid ceramic matrix infused with graphene nanoplatelets that bonds to your vehicle's clear coat. It forms a uniform protective barrier designed to boost paint clarity, enhance slickness, and resist everyday environmental accumulation.

This coating is intended to improve gloss, create high hydrophobic water-beading behavior, simplify routine washing, and guard the painted surface against everyday weathering.

At TRIEYE Detailing Studio in Villivakkam, Chennai, our detailing team ensures meticulous surface preparation and controlled application so your car or bike achieves a refined, deep gloss finish.
