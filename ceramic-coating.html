WHAT IS CERAMIC COATING?

A STRONGER SHIELD FOR YOUR PAINT

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint, creating a durable and glossy protective layer. It helps resist UV rays, contaminants, water and everyday wear, keeping your car or bike looking fresher for longer.

At TRIEYE Detailing Studio in Villivakkam, Chennai, we use high-quality ceramic coatings and a careful preparation process to give you a smooth, glossy and well-protected finish.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers. A better detailing experience.

Ceramic coating is a liquid protective coating applied to prepared vehicle paint. After curing, it creates a durable surface layer that enhances gloss, hydrophobic behaviour and ease of maintenance.

Durability depends on the coating product, preparation, vehicle usage, environmental exposure and maintenance. Proper aftercare helps the coating perform effectively for longer.

Ceramic coating can help protect the paint from light environmental contamination and make maintenance easier, but it does not make the vehicle scratch-proof or replace Paint Protection Film for physical impact protection.

Paint preparation is important before coating. Depending on the paint condition, cleaning, decontamination and paint correction may be recommended to improve the final finish before the coating is applied.

Regular gentle washing with suitable automotive products and proper drying techniques helps preserve the coating's gloss and hydrophobic performance.
